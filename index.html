Content & Funnel Strategy

Succession Story

Legacy-letter platform for attorneys · Copywriting, paid social, email, funnel diagnosis

The Brief

Succession Story is a guided questionnaire platform that helps attorneys collect meaningful legacy letters from their clients, thoughtful prompts that turn into something lasting for the client's family. The ads were working: strong click-through, healthy traffic, a clean squeeze page, and almost no one converted. That combination pointed to a message-match problem, not a design problem. The ad earned the right attention, but the landing page lost it immediately by asking people to think about "estate planning," a category most people actively avoid.

The Work

We rebuilt the entire funnel around a different pain point: emotional legacy instead of estate planning. Every piece of copy, ad hooks, squeeze page, and a three-segment email drip, was rewritten around what people actually feel uneasy about, not having said what they meant to say to the people they love. Pixel tracking was set up alongside the copy so every change could be measured against real conversion data instead of guesses.

The Result

The funnel started converting instead of leaking the traffic it had already paid for, incredibly focused on turning attention already earned into people who actually signed up, not just people who clicked.
