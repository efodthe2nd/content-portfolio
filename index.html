Content & Funnel Strategy

Succession Story

Legacy-letter SaaS · Copywriting, paid social, email, funnel diagnosis

Succession Story helps people record what they want their family to know before they're gone. I worked across content and growth for their launch funnel, handling the copy, the messaging strategy, and the infrastructure connecting the two.

The ads were working. Strong click-through rate, healthy traffic, a squeeze page that looked clean, and almost no one converted. That combination is a message-match problem, not a design problem: the ad was earning the right attention, but the landing experience lost it immediately. We were asking people to think about "estate planning," a category most people actively avoid because it's tied to death and paperwork.

We rebuilt the entire funnel around a different pain point: emotional legacy instead of estate planning. Every piece of copy, ad hooks, squeeze page, and a three-segment email drip, was rewritten around what people actually feel uneasy about: not having said what they meant to say to the people they love.

Alongside the copy, I set up and monitored pixel tracking so every change could be measured against real conversion data instead of guesses, and the funnel started converting instead of leaking the traffic it had already paid for.

Content & Writing

Notionery HQ

Social content · Copywriting, brand voice

I was part of the content and writing team behind Notionery HQ's social presence, working on the captions, copy, and voice that carried the brand across its posts.

The job wasn't just writing captions that sounded nice, it was making sure every post read like it came from the same person with the same point of view, post after post. Consistency in voice is what makes a brand feel like a brand instead of a rotating cast of writers, and that consistency is what the work was built around.

This is the same discipline I'd bring to any brand's content: a defined voice, applied consistently, so the page builds recognition instead of just posting.

Content & Paid Social

Nancy Hanson

Luxury womenswear, Lagos · Meta Ads, content strategy

Nancy Hanson is a luxury womenswear label based in Lagos. I worked with the brand on a performance partnership managing Meta Ads and content, on terms tied directly to results rather than a flat retainer.

Luxury buyers don't respond to the same content that works for mass-market fashion. The strategy was built around exclusivity, craft, and story, positioning each piece and each drop as something worth wanting, not just something worth seeing, and pairing that with paid social built to reach the right audience instead of the widest one.
